    How the clips were found

    Following the Finding References guide

    The guide's rule: the agent reads open sources and writes links for the sites only a person may open, opens every clip it keeps, and leaves the pick to you.

    1. Film-Grab and Shot Cafe stay person-only.Both sites forbid agents, so neither was browsed. Their page addresses for each film came from web search results. Where none appeared, the card links to a site search or the home page.
    2. 108 web searches for scene clips.Studio, licensed-clip, press and author uploads were preferred. Reactions, essays, multi-film compilations, fan edits and recreations were rejected, per the guide's never list.
    3. Every clip was opened.Each candidate was checked through YouTube's public oEmbed lookup: 111 checked, 93 opened, 18 were gone or refused and are dropped. Titles and channels shown here are what YouTube returned.
    4. Refs said no.The guide's open video search answered HTTP 401 on three searches. The guide treats that as final, so the link is left for you.
    5. You pick.No one has watched these clips yet. Keep or drop each one and add a note; picks are saved to this page, so Claude can read them back and turn them into a final running order.

    Where this could be wrong

    • No one has watched the clips. A clip's scene is what its YouTube title says. Check framing, burned-in text and length before the night.
    • Fan uploads can vanish at any time. Studio, licensed and author uploads are steadier.
    • The "Play block" link uses YouTube's quick-playlist address. YouTube has never documented it, so if it stops working, open the clips one by one.
    • No clip turned up for these scenes: the city walks in Her, the forest drive in After Yang, the rover drive in The Martian, the temples and rice fields in The Creator (trailers only), and the Wakanda market. Look for them in the stills sites and in your own copy.
    • Film-Grab and Shot Cafe links come from search results and were never opened, as the guide asks. Shot Cafe files Minority Report under 2007 in its address, though it is the 2002 film.
    • Both scores are editorial. The 2011 Gattaca poll was of scientists around NASA's JPL, not an official NASA position. The Martian's year (about 2035) comes from the novel. For The Creator, most sources give 2070 and one review gives 2065.
    • The palettes are drawn from memory of each film's grade, not sampled from frames.
