# Never (clip candidates)

Tests a stranger can check on the video page or the first 10 seconds of the clip.

- Reject if the title or channel says reaction, explained, review, essay, breakdown, ranking or "top 10". Prompt-side wording: the scene itself, uncut, from the film.
- Reject if the video mixes footage from more than one film (compilations, "best scenes of"). Wording: one film, one scene.
- Reject if it is a fan edit, a remake, an AI upscale with invented frames, an Unreal or Blender recreation, a lyric video or a music-video recut. Wording: original picture as released.
- Reject if burned-in text, a big channel logo or subtitles sit over the picture for most of the clip. Wording: clean frame that survives a silent projection.
- Reject if the scene is mostly dialogue in close-up with no world in frame. Wording: wide shots where the place carries the image.
- Reject if it is a darker-than-room shot for the first, brightest block of the evening. Wording: light, airy frames early; dark space frames late.

Source of these tests: what the clip searches returned (fan uploads, edits and recreations crowd the results) plus the brief.
