Mirror 2050

Open the mirror

Eight effects that went viral on X, rebuilt in one page and played with the hands. Nothing is filmed or uploaded; the camera stays in the browser.

Keys 1–8, or party mode

1 Liquid AirYour hands turn to glass and the air ripples.
2 Neon SwarmShow 1 to 4 fingers to write a word in particles; open palm for a nebula, fist for an orb.
3 Holo CubeYou become a hologram, with a glitching cube held between your hands.
4 PortalAn open palm opens a portal; pinch with both hands to tear a window into 2050.
5 ShatterAn open hand blows the picture into glass tiles; a fist pulls it back.
6 Time WarpLower rows are older frames; point a finger and time ripples from it.
7 MatrixYou turn into falling code; your hands show the real colours.
8 CloakYour body shows the room behind you. A fist decloaks.

Mars in 3D

Start Mars in 3D

Seven missions, keys 1–7

1 Pathfinder1997, Ares Vallis: the first rover, Sojourner, and its rocks.
2 Spirit2004–2010, Gusev crater: its own shadow, a dust devil, Husband Hill.
3 Opportunity2004–2018, Meridiani: blueberries, an iron meteorite, Endeavour crater.
4 Phoenix2008, the arctic: trenches dug down to ice.
5 CuriositySince 2012, Gale crater: Mount Sharp, a moving dune.
6 PerseveranceSince 2021, Jezero: Ingenuity flying, and the ground seen from it.
7 From orbitCanyons, an ancient delta, the north polar cap, Phobos.
