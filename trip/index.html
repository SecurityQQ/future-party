Varga Future Party 2050

Trip 2050

A light show for the wall. It moves with the music in the room, turns the Imagined Futures pictures into kaleidoscopes and tunnels, and brings 24 innovators on stage, from Archimedes to CRISPR.

The microphone only listens for the beat: no sound is recorded or leaves this computer.

Fast motion and flashing colours. Not for guests with photosensitive epilepsy.

Opened as a file, the browser keeps local pictures away from the graphics card: the concept art then comes from X (online only) and the innovators appear as names without portraits. For everything, offline too, run python3 -m http.server 4620 in the repo folder and open localhost:4620/trip/.
