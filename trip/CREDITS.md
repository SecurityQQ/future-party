## Visuals

- [Butterchurn](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn) 2.6.7 by Jordan Berg, a WebGL version of the MilkDrop 2 visualizer by Ryan Geiss: `vendor/butterchurn.min.js`, MIT (`vendor/LICENSE-butterchurn.txt`).
- 33 MilkDrop presets from [butterchurn-presets](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn-presets) 2.4.7, MIT (`vendor/LICENSE-butterchurn-presets.txt`). Each preset carries its authors in its name (Flexi, Geiss, martin, Aderrasi, Rovastar, Unchained, Zylot and others); `presets.json` lists the ones played.

## Pictures

The concept art is Imagined Futures (`concept-art/`): 377 pictures from 171 public posts on X. Each belongs to its artist. While a picture plays, the wall names the artist where the post does, or the account that posted it; the [Imagined Futures page](../concept-art/imagined-futures.html) links every picture to its post.

League Gothic, Geologica, Martian Mono and Unbounded, from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
