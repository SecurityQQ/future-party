# Roast of "Already Here" v1 (2026-11-14)

Judged from one frame per slide pulled from `renders/already-here-2050-1080p.mp4` (the audience view, captions included).

## The whole film

- **The thesis caps the ceiling.** "The films imagined it. Here is what already exists." A party about 2050 opens by promising the present. Every slide after it has to be a little disappointing on purpose.
- **The future shown is old.** Half the "now" items predate the iPhone: Earthrise 1968, the torus painting 1975, the Thames Barrier 1982, the Eden Project 2001, the Shanghai Maglev 2004, Cassini 2004.
- **Too many words.** 25 slides × 3 lines = 75 lines of text in 95 seconds. At a party nobody reads line three.
- **One transition, 22 times.** Every slide change is the same 0.2 s whip. By the City's fourth whip it is wallpaper.
- **Daylight grey dies on a projector.** Overcast skies, beige dirt and white rooms wash out to a pale rectangle. Big black space frames switch the wall off.
- **Forced film links.** The Wild Robot has no domes. Wanderers did not predict Cassini. 2040 is a documentary, not a vision. The Creator's robots are not a retired ISS torso.

## What v2 does instead

See `slides.json` (v2) and `CREDITS.md`. Each slide is one bold prediction with a target year, sitting on one real seed that has already happened, over the most beautiful real image of it. The real images come from observatories (ESO, Webb, CERN), NASA concept art and night photography. Nothing is grey daylight, and every film link has to be true.
