How it plays

Wall projection

Five blocks that play on their own

Each block opens with a title slide and the faces of everyone in it, then plays one tweet per slide. A slide holds for 7 to 22 seconds, timed by word count so people can read it across the room. Ten long tweets show a word-for-word excerpt on the wall. After the last block the wall starts over.

      How this list was made

      • These are all the tweets quoted on Futurists on 2050, the research page behind this one. Texts are copied from X API responses collected in November 2026, not retyped. Links that X shortens appear as “[link]”; open the tweet to follow them. Leading @mentions are shown as “Replying to”.
      • On the wall, links are left out and ten tweets longer than 65 words show an excerpt marked “Excerpt”: whole sentences taken word for word, joined by “…”. The list below shows every tweet in full.
      • Every tweet mentions 2050, except Vinod Khosla’s numbered thread items, which follow his “Here are my predictions for 2050”, and Peter Diamandis on Ray Kurzweil, which is about the 2040s.
      • “Own forecast”, “Conditional or goal” and “Cites others” are the labels from the research page. “Cites others” means the author passes on someone else’s forecast or a scenario.
      • Names are written plainly; on X some display names carry emoji, hashtags or other scripts. Each role line is a short summary of the person’s X bio and public role.
      • Profile pictures are the ones on X on 14 November 2026. Likes and views are as of collection and will have changed.
