ABOUT THIS JOURNEY

A little context.
A different way to hear.

This is a curated route through European classical music and several jazz, blues, rock, hip-hop, dance, and experimental electronic traditions. It is not a complete or global history of music.

Every stop connects a recording to its context, explains a musical change, and offers something concrete to listen for. These traditions overlap and exchange ideas. No single work invented emotion, rhythm, or “the future.”

Music of the Future

The Future Lab adds 12 concepts and 27 distinct references. Its 2040 scenarios are thought experiments, not predictions. Sources, verified production techniques and uncertain upload metadata are separated from listening interpretations. The original history route remains intact.

Explore at your own pace

Select a chapter or timeline stop. Use the arrow keys to move between stops when you are not typing, and press / to search. Mark a stop as listened or save it for later. Progress stays in this browser; no account is needed.

Playback & privacy

Recordings play through YouTube. Nothing is downloaded, and the app does not contact YouTube until you press play. When opened as a local HTML file, playback opens on YouTube. Serve the app over HTTP or HTTPS to use the in-page player.

Availability can change. “Open on YouTube” and “Find another recording” remain available beside every example. Some videos contain explicit language or imagery.

Dates identify works, premieres, recording sessions, or releases—not the upload dates of selected performances. Some examples are later performances or official videos rather than the original record. Source links and recording notes are included at each stop.
