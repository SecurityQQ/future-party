# Mars in 3D: credits

Every picture is a stereo pair from a spacecraft, published as a red/cyan anaglyph by its agency. NASA pictures are public domain (NASA's media guidelines ask for the credit line given); ESA pictures are licensed [CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/igo/). The pictures in `img/` are cropped to cut away black mosaic edges and resized; nothing else is changed.

## The globe

- Colour: [Mars Viking MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_1km.jpg), NASA/JPL/USGS, public domain.
- Relief: [MOLA gridded topography](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mgs/megdr.html) (MEGT90N000CB, 4 pixels per degree), NASA/GSFC MOLA Science Team, public domain. The globe exaggerates it.
- Rendering: [three.js](https://threejs.org) r147, MIT licence (`vendor/three.min.js`, `vendor/LICENSE-three.txt`). The red/cyan mix uses Eric Dubois' least-squares anaglyph matrices, as three.js's AnaglyphEffect does.
