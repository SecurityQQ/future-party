Mars in 3D

Real stereo pictures of Mars, for red/cyan 3D glasses with the red lens on the left eye. Every picture was taken by a spacecraft on or above Mars, by two cameras side by side or by one camera from two places, and published as an anaglyph by NASA or ESA. Between the missions a live globe of Mars, coloured with the Viking mosaic and raised with laser-altimeter heights (ten times higher than real), flies to each landing site. Back to the party’s apps
